# ADR-0007: Standards Sourcing Cites ITU-T, ETSI, ISO/IEC and NIST Only

_Status: Accepted_

## Context

The seed material references a vendor-neutral standards landscape and an
industry specifications forum.

## Decision

Published artefacts cite ITU-T M.3050.x, ETSI technical specifications,
ISO/IEC standards and NIST publications only. References to the industry
specifications forum and its published artefacts are not carried into any
artefact, in any spelling or abbreviation, including in the source notes
from which the seed documents were derived.

## Rationale

The programme's authority must rest on open, citable standards rather than
on a single vendor's specifications. Naming the specifications forum would
imply the concept's standing derives from that forum's work. It does not.
It derives from the reasoning, which is the programme's own.

## Consequences

Source notes derived from the seed documents are rephrased rather than
quoted where they would carry the reference. The checklist gate L1 enforces
this on every published artefact.
