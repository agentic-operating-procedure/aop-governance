# ADR-0010: Exemplars Are a Separate Repository, Deferred to Wave 6

_Status: Accepted_

## Context

Worked examples are the clearest demonstration that the concept is real.
Two are planned: a fault management procedure and a customer refund
procedure.

## Decision

A separate `aop-examples` repository is planned but deferred to wave 6.

## Rationale

An exemplar is worth nothing until the parts it demonstrates exist and
conform. Building the exemplar first would produce a narrative that the
later implementation has to be bent to fit, which is precisely the failure
mode the programme argues against in SOP-derived procedures.

## Consequences

The repository appears in the estate from the start so that the ladder and
the board reflect the complete target shape, but it is not created until
wave 6. The estate is incomplete until it lands.
