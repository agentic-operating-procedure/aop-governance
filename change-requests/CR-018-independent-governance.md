## Problem

CRs, ADRs, findings, release policy and templates exist with an explicit programme-owner approval model. Definitions, tests and claims currently rest on the same stewards who author and certify the reference artefacts; independent review, conflict handling, dissent recording, appeals and claim withdrawal are not established.

## Change

Define reviewer eligibility, conflicts of interest, quorum and dissent recording; require at least one implementer or domain reviewer outside the authoring path for normative changes; independent evidence review for certification and evaluation claims; public claims citing version, profile, cohort, protocol, result and limitations (the reproducibility protocol already requires most of this; extend to claims discipline); a correction and withdrawal process; documented stewardship succession.

## Acceptance

- A reviewer can require a claim to be narrowed or withdrawn, with the process recorded.
- Proportionate for a draft standard while credible enough for external readers.
