# CR-019: Risk-Tiered Adoption and Promotion Gates

## Problem

The six-stage playbook, the maturity model and the three scenarios exist and are credited. What is missing is the alignment layer: per-action-class risk tiers driving a progressive operating model (observe-only, recommend, approval-gated, bounded, expanded) with measurable promotion, demotion and emergency-stop criteria tied to independent evidence, and a clean distinction between a conformant AOP and an AOP approved for a specific risk tier.

## Change

Define per-action-class operating modes with entry and exit gates, required monitoring and evidence per mode; promotion requiring independent evaluation against predeclared thresholds (elapsed time alone is insufficient); measurable demotion and stop criteria; templates distinguishing conformance from operational authorisation per tier; cross-reference the CR-009-3 budgets as the cumulative-control mechanism inside each tier.

## Acceptance

- A low-risk workflow is useful in observe or recommend mode while evidence accumulates.
- Failures that trigger immediate demotion or suspension are enumerated and tested.
- Model confidence is never a proxy for authority.
